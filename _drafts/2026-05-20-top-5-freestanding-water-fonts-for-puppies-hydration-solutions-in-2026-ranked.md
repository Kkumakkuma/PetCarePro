## What Most Guides Get Wrong

### Focusing Only on Price

Many guides suggest that cheaper fountains work just as well as premium options. Why this matters: I quickly learned that a more expensive fountain may save you money long-term by requiring less maintenance and fewer filter replacements. I bought a $25 model that broke after a month—a waste compared to a $60 option that lasted for years.

### Overlooking Maintenance

Some lists omit the ease of cleaning and maintaining the fountains. Why this matters: I faced challenges with a highly complex fountain that required disassembly every week, leading me to favor models with dishwasher-safe parts. Easy cleaning not only saves time but also helps prevent mold or bacteria growth.

### Neglecting Flow Rate and Design

A fountain’s design and flow rate greatly influence how pets interact with it. Why this matters: I once purchased a fountain that looked great but had minimal flow. My pup ignored it, leading to decreased hydration levels. A good design and adjustable flow can make your pet more inclined to drink.
