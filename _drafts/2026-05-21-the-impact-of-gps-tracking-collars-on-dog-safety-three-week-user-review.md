## How Does GPS Tracking Work?

GPS tracking collars use Global Positioning System (GPS) technology to determine the location of your pet in real-time. By connecting to satellite signals, these collars can transmit location data to your smartphone or computer. Most models come with mobile applications that offer features like geofencing, activity tracking, and health monitoring.

These devices typically contain a small GPS receiver, a cellular transmitter, and a rechargeable battery. The GPS receiver calculates your dog's location, while the cellular transmitter communicates this information to the app on your device.

Absolutely, but keep in mind that signal quality may fluctuate based on your location. Dense urban structures like high buildings could affect accuracy. It's advisable to familiarize yourself with how the device works in various settings.

Yes, most GPS collars require a smartphone or tablet to access fully their tracking capabilities through an app. Be sure the collar you choose is compatible with your device.
